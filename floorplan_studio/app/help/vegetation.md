Add a **Plant**, **Planter** or **Tree** from the furniture palette, then choose
its **Look**. Each is drawn from above, as a crown or planting footprint.

## Tree looks

| Look | Useful for |
|---|---|
| Deciduous | General rounded garden trees |
| Pine | Fine radial evergreen needles |
| Palm | Simple narrow palm fronds |
| Flowering | A rounded canopy with separate blossoms |
| Broadleaf | Dense overlapping leaf masses |
| Spreading | Broad shade trees with a wide, layered crown |
| Weeping | Long curved branches radiating toward the crown edge |
| Columnar | Compact upright-tree planting footprints |
| Fruit | Orchard trees with separately coloured fruit |
| Coconut | Feathered fronds and a central coconut cluster |
| Fan palm | Broad pleated fan canopy |
| Banana | Broad leaves with lateral veins |
| Bamboo | A clump of stems with fine leaf sprays |
| Bare | Branch structure without foliage |

## Plant and planter looks

**Potted, bush, succulent, fern, flowering and monstera** cover the original
houseplants and shrubs. **Aloe, snake plant, ornamental grass and cactus** add
pointed rosettes, narrow leaves, fine grass blades and ribbed cactus clusters.
**Hedge** and **flower bed** fill rectangular planting strips; **lotus** combines
round pads and a central bloom; **bonsai** includes a shallow container and
separate clipped crowns.

## Size and colour

**Width** and **Depth** are the planting's plan spread, not its height. Look
presets range from a one-foot succulent to an 18 × 14 ft spreading crown.
These are editable illustration defaults, not species-specific mature sizes
or planting-clearance recommendations. The editor preserves dimensions you
have set yourself when switching looks.

Use **Colour scheme** for foliage: the Garden group offers **Leaf green,
Evergreen, Silver sage and Autumn canopy**. You can also create a project-owned
scheme. **Flower colour** changes petals on flowering plants/trees, cactus,
flower beds and lotus. **Fruit colour** changes the fruit-tree crop. Flowers
keep gold centres; coconut clusters and the bonsai container have their own
natural brown details.

Colour schemes and individual colours travel with the project. The same
drawings appear in the editor, SVG exports and generated dashboard card.
