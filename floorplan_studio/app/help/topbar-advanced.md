## Floors

Floors are tabs. Add one with **+**; each carries its own level height, its own
extent and its own icon. A floor's **level** is what stacks them in the house
overview, so it is worth setting even though nothing on the plan uses it.

## Theme

A theme decides the plan's colours. Two things it sets are different in kind:
the **floor background** must be a real colour, because a flooring generator
shades that colour to derive its own grain and grout and cannot shade a
variable. Everything else follows your Home Assistant theme.

## Advanced

Most panels hide their less-used settings behind the **Advanced** tick. With it
off, a panel carries what a plan actually needs; with it on, everything the type
declares.

When settings are hidden, the panel says how many — so a control you remember is
never simply missing, and you are never left hunting for something that is one
tick away.

## Zoom to fit

Worth pressing after adding a floor or importing a plan. It is also the fastest
way to find something you placed by accident a long way outside the house.
